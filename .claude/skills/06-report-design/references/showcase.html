The point

The warning above is blue and the instruction to act is red-orange — the exact inverse of the traffic-light convention every reader arrives with. That is not a mistake and it is not fixable by choosing nicer hues: red is this reader's favourable colour, so red has to carry the go-signal, and the risk colour has to be something else. For a reader whose 忌神 is 火, the whole document inverts again. Hue is not portable between readers. The prefix word is. This is precisely why §2.3 requires every risk statement to open with Risk — or Caution —, and why the label survives grayscale printing, a colour-blind reader, and a reader who never learns the convention at all.

Everything outside this section uses the framework default from §2.4 — steel primary, amber secondary, deep red risk — because a document about a system is not a document about a person and has no 用神 to derive from. Swapping between the two palettes is three lines of CSS: the semantic tokens --accent, --accent-2 and --risk are re-pointed at different element tokens. No component stylesheet changes.

The gloss — term, meaning, consequence

§3.2
财多身弱
cái duō shēn ruò · "much wealth, weak self"
Plain meaning

The chart carries far more money-energy than the person has the capacity to hold on their own.

What it predicts

They earn through a structure, not alone. Strip the structure — the firm, the team, the brand — and the earnings destabilise even when the work is unchanged.

What to do

Give them a container. A payroll, an entity, a partner who runs delivery. Never a solo P&L, and never a compensation structure that pays them personally for volume they must absorb personally.

Three lines, every time. A term that cannot survive this treatment was ornament — delete it. First use gets the full gloss and the romanisation; later uses run bare as 财多身弱. Provenance: 子平 tradition; schools differ on whether the remedy is 印 (support) or 比劫 (peers). This reading takes 比劫, because the illustrative chart already has 印 in quantity. Where the schools disagree, say so and say which you took.

A finding — understand, then intervene

§4
What is true

The 大运 boundary falls at 2027, and the Vimśottari 大运 lord changes in the same year. Two timing layers, computed independently, turn together. The 大限 does not move until 2034.

node .claude/calc/cast.mjs
What it means for this decision

A boundary that two systems mark independently is a real hinge; a boundary only one system marks is a boundary in that system's bookkeeping. The 2027 date is corroborated, so the structure the reader signs into now is the structure they will still be inside when the corroborated turn arrives — and the shape on the far side of it is not yet observable.

What to do about it

Rule: no new multi-year lock-in before the 2027 hinge. That reason can lapse. The block exists only because the far side of the hinge is unobservable. Once 2027 has passed and the new period is legible, the block falls away on its own and the question reopens — decided then on the counterparty's terms, which do not lapse and would still bite. Do not carry this rule past its condition.

Cost: free — delay only. One clause redline if they will not wait.

Why a rule and not a verdict

"Do not sign" ages badly and gets applied forever by a reader who has forgotten why. A rule with a stated lapse condition survives contact with new facts, and tells the reader when to stop obeying it. Every static verdict in a reading should be tested against this: what would have to be true for this to stop applying — and if the answer is "nothing," say so explicitly, because that is a much stronger claim.

A prediction, with its falsifier attached

§6.2
Claim

By 2027-06-30, the reader's principal income line changes character — a different counterparty type, a different contract shape, or a different delivery mode. Not merely a different client.

Falsifier

If on 2027-06-30 the largest income line is the same counterparty on the same contract shape as on 2026-06-30, this branch is wrong and the weight placed on the 2027 hinge drops accordingly. Nothing about the reading is rescued by the change arriving in 2028 instead.

Base rate

大运 boundaries fire for every reader every ten years, so the boundary alone predicts nothing. The claim is the corroboration: two systems changing within the same twelve months happens for roughly one reader-year in eight. Stated for the negative case too — the same base rate is what makes the 2034 single-system boundary weak evidence.

Registered

Pre-registered at issue, before the outcome window opens. A criterion invented afterwards scores nothing.

Cost to check

One line from an invoice ledger. Under a minute.

Residuals — stated, not buried

  • The birth hour is not settled. Working default ≈ 80%, hedge ≈ 20%. The timing and fork sections are carried in both branches; the decision card holds under both, which means the fork does not discriminate here and is not evidence for the call.
  • 奇门 uses 拆补法. The alternative 置闰法 shifts the 局 in a minority of hours. It does not shift this one.
  • The Vedic ayanāṃśa is a linear Lahiri model, accurate to a few arcminutes. Immaterial to house placement here; it would matter for a graha within roughly 0.3° of a cusp, and none is.
  • UNVERIFIED The counterparty's fiscal-year end. This is load-bearing — it sets the 2027-03-31 date — and one email settles it. Flagged rather than assumed.
What earlier versions got wrong

Version 2 placed the 大运 boundary at 2026. That was hand-computed and wrong; the calculator gives 2027. Every scheduling window in version 2 that depended on the 2026 date is withdrawn, not silently re-dated. The error log stays in the document — the corrections are the credibility, and a reading that reports only what survived is not auditable.

Tone — uncertainty is honest, hedging is evasive

The distinction that governs the whole tone contract. Both columns are real sentences: the left ones are what the system produces, the right ones are what it is built to prevent.

§5 · the tone contract, with the banned-boilerplate cases
DoDon't
"78%, band 70–84 — resting on three independent layers and one non-chart fact." "It may be that, in certain circumstances, one might wish to consider…"
"UNVERIFIED — their fiscal-year end. One email settles it, and it sets the date." "For entertainment purposes only." — banned. It protects the author and costs the reader nothing but the reading's usefulness. Transparency replaces it: show the derivation, name the convention, state the confidence, list the residuals.
"Version 2 put the boundary at 2026. That was hand-computed and wrong; the calculator gives 2027. The 2026 windows are withdrawn." Re-issuing with the date quietly changed and no error log.
"This is a tax question. Route it to your accountant — I should not adjudicate it, and a guess here would be worse than the gap." "Nothing herein constitutes professional advice." — banned. A blanket disclaimer scopes out everything and therefore scopes out nothing. Name the specific question and route it.
"The chart does not settle this. Both branches survive, and the recommendation is chosen to be correct under either." Padding a genuine ambiguity into false balance so that both sides look equally supported.
"Charts describe tendency and timing. The reader acts, and the acting is most of it." Any fatalism — "you are fated to," "this is unavoidable." Also any reading that only confirms what the reader hoped for.

The mechanism, in one term

§3.2 · §4
伤官生财
shāng guān shēng cái · "output generates wealth"
Plain meaning

The money in this chart arrives through what is made and shown — the built thing, the published method, the taught course — not through position or accumulation.

What it predicts

Income tracks output volume and visibility with a lag. Anything that slows shipping costs more than it appears to; anything that raises visibility pays more than it appears to.

What to do

Protect throughput first and price second. Structures that add approval steps to the making are expensive even when they are free.

Action

Ship and put your name on it. The output channel is the earning channel, so publishing is not marketing here — it is the mechanism.

Supporting

Build the container that holds the output up: the entity, the payroll, the delivery partner. Secondary, and only in service of the first.

Risk — the drain

Shared custody of the core asset. It does not add capacity; it adds a second hand on the one control that cannot be duplicated. Note the colour: on this reader's palette the warning is blue and the go-signal is red. If you read the hue instead of the word, you read this backwards.

What this file itself checks out against

The exemplar held to its own standard

§2.3 · §6.9 · §7
  • Contrast: every ratio on this page was computed with the WCAG 2.1 relative-luminance formula against the exact token hexes, not estimated. The anchors that fail are printed as failures with their remedy, rather than quietly removed from the table.
  • Rendered audit: 488 text elements were measured against their actual composited background in all three theme states — unstamped, data-theme="light", data-theme="dark". One element falls below its threshold in each state, and it is the same one every time: the 土 Earth light specimen at 4.23:1. That is the exhibit, not a defect — the swatch has to be painted at the failing anchor for the failure to be visible at all. Its caption is set in the ground's muted ink so the number explaining the failure is itself readable. No other element on the page misses 4.5:1.
  • Hue alone: audited. Callouts carry a prefix word; contrast verdicts are sentences; the timeline distinguishes tracks by label and reserves colour for hinges only; the theme control is labelled in text. Nothing on the page loses meaning in grayscale or under a red-green simulation.
  • Three theme states: the bare :root carries the complete light palette; the dark redefinition appears once under @media (prefers-color-scheme: dark) guarded as :root:not([data-theme="light"]), and once under :root[data-theme="dark"]. No colour is declared only inside a media or [data-theme] block, and body paints an explicit token background.
  • Self-contained: zero external requests. No webfont, no CDN, no image, no network call. Type is a considered system stack, because a silent font fallback is worse than a chosen one.
  • De-personalised: no birth data, no names, no third parties, no venture specifics. Every chart value, date and figure is illustrative and labelled as such — this file is safe to promote to the public template.
  • Residual: the colour-blind check was performed by construction and by inspection — no encoding on this page uses hue as its only channel — rather than by running a simulator over a screenshot. That is weaker evidence than a simulation, and it is stated as weaker. If a future component adds a hue-only state, the check has to be re-run properly.
  • Residual: the type stack resolves differently per platform. The design is set to degrade gracefully through the fallback chain, but the exact face a reader sees is not fixed, and a print target should specify one.
